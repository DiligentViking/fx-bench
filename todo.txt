DESCRIPTION:

-"FX Bench: Small, useful, hand-coded effects for websites."

-This is a collection of (relatively) small effects for hover, click, and logos.

-I've always loved button hover effects.  Ever since I was a child with semi-primitive UI's I would like to explore every little change that happened when I hovered and clicked on stuff.

-I've surfed around and most collections of effects on the web have friction points.

-They are largely made up of superfluously special, creative, or just very large-scale effects and animations.  Now it's impressive that those people made such wondrous displays, but it would look really wacky for actual websites to have those, except highly niche ones.
-The best effects are clear to understand and they seem simple.  A professional favors small yet polished interactions.

-They use codepen for everything.
-Mine will be all loaded there so you don't have to scroll past codepen boxes one by one.

-They don't even show the effects sometimes, forcing you to click a link instead.
-My effects are all loaded right there for you to interact with.

-They're paid.
-Mine are open.

-And I try not to have those plain strange/ugly animations that remind you of a windows-xp screen saver.
